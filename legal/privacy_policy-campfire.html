General overview

This page is used to inform you regarding my policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information if anyone decided to use my service.

Campfire is an offline app for viewing and editing song lyrics and chords. Your songs and setlists are ordinary ChordPro text files kept in the app's own storage on your device, and on the web in your browser's private storage for the site. There is no Campfire account and there is no server of mine involved anywhere: I never receive your songs, your settings or anything else about how you use the app.

Campfire contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind.

Information collection and use

Campfire does not have any data collection solution integrated, as a result, I am not collecting any personally identifiable information on purpose. However, keep in mind that some data collection mechanisms are beyond my control. For instance, just by downloading the application from the Google Play Store, you are automatically sharing some limited usage data and crash logs with me, the developer, as well as Google. You agreed to this when accepting the Play Services license agreement, over which I have no control whatsoever.

Cloud sync

Campfire can keep your library the same on several of your own devices by storing it in a cloud folder that belongs to you. This is switched off until you turn it on. Until you connect an account in the app's settings, Campfire makes no network requests at all, and if you never connect one, nothing in this section applies to you.

Sync currently supports Dropbox. When you connect it:

  • Your files go to your own Dropbox, not to me. The songs and setlists are transferred directly between your device and Dropbox. I operate no server, and no copy of your library ever reaches me.
  • Campfire can only see its own folder. The app requests Dropbox's "app folder" permission, which limits its access to a single folder called Apps/Campfire that is created for it. The rest of your Dropbox is invisible to the app.
  • Only your library is synced. That means your song files and your setlist files. Your app settings, chosen theme and language, text size and transpositions are never uploaded and stay on the device where you set them.
  • What Campfire reads about your account. After you connect, the app asks Dropbox for the display name and email address of the account, purely so that the settings screen can show you which account your library is going to. This is displayed on your device and is never sent anywhere else.
  • How signing in works. Authorization uses the standard OAuth 2.0 flow with PKCE, on Dropbox's own consent page opened in your browser. Your Dropbox password is never typed into Campfire and never passes through it. The resulting access and refresh tokens are stored in the app's private storage on your device only.
  • Disconnecting. Choosing "Disconnect" in the settings asks Dropbox to revoke the token and deletes it from your device. Your files are left untouched, both on the device and in your Dropbox; you can also remove Campfire's access at any time from the connected applications page of your Dropbox account.

Anything stored in your Dropbox is subject to Dropbox's own privacy policy and terms, which are between you and Dropbox. Should Campfire gain support for another cloud service in the future, the same principles will apply to it and this page will be updated to name it.
